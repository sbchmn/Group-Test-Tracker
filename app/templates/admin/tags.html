Unused tags are listed first because removing them affects nothing. Renaming a tag changes how the dashboard groups and sorts tests, so sections may move. A tag that is in use cannot be deleted — rename it, merge it into the spelling you want to keep, or retire it to take it out of the picker. Retire and Hide from bots are separate on purpose: retiring stops a spelling being offered to admins while leaving public menus untouched, and hiding trims the bot menu while leaving the results published. The bot list only ever shows tags that have at least one published result, so retiring junk does not shorten it.
